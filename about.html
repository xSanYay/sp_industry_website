About SP Industries

Est. 1995 | Bengaluru, India

SP Industries (formerly M/s Shri Parameshwari Enterprises) was established in 1995 as a manufacturer of precision test and measuring instruments for the electrical power industry.

We design and manufacture testing equipment under the brand name 'AMBER' and serve customers across power generation, transmission, and distribution industries.

Industries We Serve

  • Power generation companies
  • Transmission and distribution utilities
  • Transformer and motor manufacturers
  • Electrical equipment testing laboratories
  • Industrial maintenance divisions
